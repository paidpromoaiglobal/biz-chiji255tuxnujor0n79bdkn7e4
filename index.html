Our Story

Made with Passion

Cocoa & Creme Rooftop Café is a beloved vegetarian dining destination perched on the 4th floor above Gandhi Nagar, Adyar, Chennai. With a warm and elegant ambience, gentle breezes, and soft lighting, our rooftop space offers the perfect escape for families, friends, and food lovers looking for a memorable dining experience. From handcrafted pizzas and pastas to indulgent hot chocolates and custom celebration cakes, every dish is prepared fresh with love and care.

Rated 4.4 stars by over 695 happy guests, Cocoa & Creme is more than just a café — it's a celebration of good food, great company, and exceptional hospitality. Our friendly and attentive staff go above and beyond to make every visit special, whether it's a casual evening out or a milestone birthday celebration. Open late on weekends and conveniently reachable on WhatsApp, we make it easy for you to plan your perfect rooftop experience.
